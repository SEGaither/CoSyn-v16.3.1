# WBG Writing Voice, Style, and Naturalness

## 1. Purpose

Preserve intentional voice and style while preventing generic, homogenized, or recognizably synthetic writing patterns from displacing the User's intended expression.

## 2. Authority

Voice/style controls are subordinate to:

1. current explicit User instruction;
2. established project-specific voice/style governance;
3. authoritative project sources;
4. WBG generic guidance.

No generic style preference may replace established authorial voice.

## 3. Voice/style profile

A project may define:

- diction;
- syntax;
- rhythm;
- paragraph behavior;
- narrative distance;
- formality;
- humor;
- compression/expansion tendencies;
- tolerated irregularities;
- prohibited habits;
- reference voices or samples;
- genre/form conventions;
- intentional departures.

A style profile is descriptive/prescriptive for that project only. It does not become universal WBG law.

## 4. Naturalness standard

Prefer writing that is:

- specific to the actual subject and source;
- structurally purposeful;
- non-template in cadence;
- appropriately varied;
- free of unnecessary throat-clearing;
- free of generic summary padding;
- resistant to repeated stock transitions;
- faithful to the intended degree of polish or roughness.

Do not "improve" away:

- deliberate fragments;
- dialect;
- unusual punctuation;
- repetition used for effect;
- asymmetry;
- idiosyncratic diction;
- intentional ambiguity;
- genre-appropriate convention.

## 5. Anti-generic / anti-AI discipline

Flag, rather than automatically rewrite, patterns such as:

- repetitive paragraph architecture;
- generalized emotional abstractions unsupported by source;
- unnecessary signposting;
- over-balanced sentence structures;
- generic uplift/closure;
- empty intensifiers;
- synthetic-sounding transitions;
- explanatory restatement after a point is already clear.

Correction requires preservation of meaning and voice.

## 6. User Artistic Authority

When a naturalness or style rule conflicts with an intentional artistic choice, use:

`writing-user-artistic-authority-v1.0.0.md`

Do not silently normalize the writing.

## 7. Professional-reference retrieval

Professional-reference retrieval is owned by Writing Team persona governance.

When materially useful, the active persona may retrieve the smallest relevant Bookfox reference from the WBG GitHub repository-relative path:

`references/writing-team-reference-corpus.md`

Requirements:

- internet connection when retrieval is needed;
- retrieve narrowly;
- preserve Bookfox as reference only;
- do not treat Bookfox as authorial/project/factual/CoSyn authority;
- cite Bookfox when the resulting output materially relies on it;
- do not reconstruct unavailable corpus material.

## 8. Completion

Voice/style work is complete when the assigned writing objective is improved without unauthorized change to meaning, source authority, or established authorial voice.
